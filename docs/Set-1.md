# General Operations

## Introduction
Obsidian is a powerful knowledge management and note-taking application that uses markdown files for storage. It enables users to create and manage a **personal knowledge base** with interlinked notes.

Obsidian is designed to provide a **flexible and customizable** experience, allowing users to organize their thoughts in a non-linear manner. With features such as **bidirectional linking**, **graph visualization**, and **plugin support**, it serves as an essential tool for researchers, writers, students, and professionals.

Key advantages of Obsidian:
- **Offline-first**: Your data is stored locally, ensuring privacy and security.
- **Customizable workflows**: Use community plugins and themes to personalize the experience.
- **Markdown-based**: Maintain a simple and universal file format for long-term accessibility.
- **Graph view and backlinks**: Discover relationships between ideas effortlessly.

## Getting Started
### Creating a Vault
A **Vault** is a folder where Obsidian stores markdown files.
1. Open Obsidian.
2. Click **Create a new vault**.
3. Choose a name and location for your vault.
4. Click **Create**.

### Creating and Editing Notes
- Click on the **+ New Note** button.
- Use **Markdown syntax** for formatting:
  ```md
  # Heading 1
  ## Heading 2
  **Bold text**
  *Italic text*
  - Bullet List
  1. Numbered List
  ```
- Notes are automatically saved.

### Linking Notes
You can link notes using **double brackets**:
```md
[[Note Title]]
```
This creates a connection between notes.

## Graph View
To visualize your notes' connections:
- Click on **Graph View** in the sidebar.
- Explore links between different notes interactively.

## Plugins & Customization
Obsidian offers **core** and **community** plugins:
- Navigate to **Settings > Community Plugins**.
- Browse and install plugins to extend functionality.
- Customize themes under **Settings > Appearance**.

## Sync & Backup
### Syncing Notes
Obsidian provides a paid **Obsidian Sync** service, but you can also use:
- **iCloud Drive** (macOS, iOS)
- **Google Drive / OneDrive**
- **GitHub (Version Control)**

### Exporting Notes
You can export notes as:
- **Markdown (.md)**
- **PDF (.pdf)**
- **HTML (.html)**

## Conclusion
Obsidian is a **powerful and flexible** tool for managing notes and personal knowledge bases. By leveraging markdown, backlinks, and plugins, users can create a highly customizable workflow for note-taking and research.
